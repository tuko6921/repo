## 📍 About BIS (Base Information Services)

**Base Information Services (BIS)** is a service organization within **DND** (Department of National Defence) that supports naval communication both at sea and ashore.

### 📁 Departments Within BIS

BIS consists of three departments:

1. **Operation Infrastructure Support (OIS)**  
2. **Software Development Centre (SDC)**  
3. **Operations Department**

Each department has several teams.

---

## 🧑‍💻 About Labour Unions

- Most full-time civilian employees are part of a **labour union**
- The majority belong to the **IT Group**, a subgroup of:
  - **The Professional Institute of the Public Service Canada (PIPSC)**
  - This union spans the **entire federal government**, not just DND

### ❗ Student & Casual Status

- As a **student**, you are **not part of a labour union**
- Casual and term employees are not unionized either
